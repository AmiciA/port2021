Escape from PolyU

PHOTOSENSITIVITY WARNING: flashing lights; game runs at 10fps

Depicting the flight of a Hong Kong protester from the besieged Polytechnic University in November 2019. Traverse the sewer tunnels below the Kowloon streets and pray to surface in safety. 4 potential 'endings', but you can always go back down...

This was made for Spooky Screensaver Jam in October 2020, so there's no player input beyond starting the run.
