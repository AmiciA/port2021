My Hong Kong

After moving to the US from Hong Kong in 2021, I attempted a return trip in 2024. Unfortunately, I was detained in the airport and denied entry due to █████████████ ██ ████████ ██ ████.

Kicked back to the US, my 2-week holiday instead turned into 2-weeks off work with nothing much to do. So I made this zine while processing the grief of not being allowed back to the first place I'd really built a home for myself.
